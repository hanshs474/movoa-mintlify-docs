[Movoa](https://movoa.ai/?utm_source=mintlify&utm_medium=docs) makes short video clips with sound from a prompt ([text to video](https://movoa.ai/video/text-to-video)) or a photo ([photo to video](https://movoa.ai/video/photo-to-video)).

## Quick start

- **API keys**: create one at [movoa.ai/settings/apikeys](https://movoa.ai/settings/apikeys) and send `Authorization: Bearer sk-...`.
- **Pricing**: runs cost credits at the prices on [movoa.ai/pricing](https://movoa.ai/pricing).
- **Models**: `minimax-h3` (default), `veo-3-1-fast`, `seedance-2-5`, `seedance-2`, `seedance-2-fast`, `seedance-2-mini`. Every clip includes synchronized audio.
